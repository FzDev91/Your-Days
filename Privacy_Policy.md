## 2. Data We Collect

### 2.1 Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Your Days stores the following data **exclusively on your device** using a local SQLite database. This data is **never transmitted to our servers**:

- Habit names, categories, and schedules you create
- Habit completion records and streaks
- App preferences (language, theme, religion)
- Notification settings

We do not have access to this data. It never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it using the built-in Export feature.

### 2.2 Data Collected by Third-Party Services

The App integrates the following third-party services that may collect limited data:

#### a) Google AdMob (Advertising)
- Used to display advertisements in the **free version** of the App
- May collect: Device advertising ID, approximate location, device information, app usage data
- Used for: Serving relevant ads and measuring ad performance
- **Premium subscribers are not shown ads**
- Privacy policy: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

#### b) RevenueCat (Subscription Management)
- Used to manage premium subscriptions and verify purchase status
- May collect: Anonymous app user ID, device identifiers, purchase history (no payment card data)
- Payment processing is handled entirely by **Google Play** — RevenueCat never sees your full payment information
- Privacy policy: [https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy)

#### c) Google Play Billing (In-App Purchases)
- Handles all subscription payment processing
- We do not store your payment information. All financial data is managed by Google
- Privacy policy: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

#### d) expo-notifications (Local Push Notifications)
- Used to send daily habit reminders (if enabled by you)
- Notifications are **scheduled locally on your device** using the operating system's built-in scheduler
- **No internet connection is required** — notifications fire even in airplane mode
- **No data is sent to any server** for notification delivery
- No external accounts or services are involved

Kami tidak memiliki akses ke data ini. Data tidak pernah meninggalkan perangkatmu kecuali kamu secara sengaja mengekspornya menggunakan fitur Ekspor.

### 2.2 Data yang Dikumpulkan oleh Layanan Pihak Ketiga

Aplikasi mengintegrasikan layanan pihak ketiga berikut yang mungkin mengumpulkan data terbatas:

- Kebijakan privasi: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

#### b) RevenueCat (Manajemen Langganan)
- Digunakan untuk mengelola langganan premium dan memverifikasi status pembelian

- Pemrosesan pembayaran sepenuhnya dilakukan oleh **Google Play** — RevenueCat tidak pernah melihat informasi pembayaranmu secara lengkap
- Kebijakan privasi: [https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy)

#### c) Google Play Billing (Pembelian Dalam Aplikasi)

- Kebijakan privasi: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

#### d) expo-notifications (Notifikasi Push Lokal)
- Digunakan untuk mengirim pengingat kebiasaan harian (jika diaktifkan olehmu)

- Tidak ada akun atau layanan eksternal yang terlibat
